AboutThe studio

A studiowith one core.

Think beyond. Build without limits.
HYDRA is an independent digital development studio. We take a product at any stage — an idea, a half-built prototype or a live site that needs a new life — and turn it into a fast, considered, working digital experience.
01Philosophy

Think beyond
the template

The name

In the myth, the hydra grows two heads for every one that is cut off. We took the idea literally: one core — research, design and engineering working as one — and four heads, each a discipline that grows from that core.

A limitation in a brief is not a stop sign for us. It is the moment to look for the better solution, and then to build it properly.

02Approach

How we
think

01

Evidence

We study users, the existing product and strong references before drawing anything. Our Design Intelligence tool keeps that research systematic.

02

Systems

We design systems — type, colour, grid, motion, components — instead of one-off pages. That is what keeps a product consistent as it grows.

03

Iteration

Small releases, real content, early feedback. A working staging link beats a slide deck.

03Specialisation

Four heads

Design, UI/UX and the visual concept are part of every one of them — not separate products.

04Values

What we
hold to

01

Focus

Four services, done properly. Saying no to everything else is how we keep the quality of those four.

02

Honesty

Demo projects are labelled as demos. Estimates are scoped. If something will not work, we say so early.

03

Craft

Typography, motion and code get the same attention. Details are where users decide to trust a product.

04

Performance

Fast on a mid-range phone, not only on our machines. Effects that cost speed must earn their place.

05

Accessibility

Keyboard, screen readers, contrast and reduced motion are part of the definition of done.

06

Ownership

You own the code and the content. Everything is documented so any developer can continue the work.
