Coffee House & Bakery

StatusDemo
CategoryWebsites · Redesign
ClientExample — fictional coffee house in Podil, Kyiv
Year2026
timur-alt2008.github.io/ProCoffee-For-Example-For-my-Portfolio/
Open live demoSource on GitHub
Overview

A five-page website for a European-style coffee house built around the idea of “coffee in your hands”.

Goal

Rebuild the original single-page coffee site into a full multi-page experience with a menu, story, gallery and table booking.

Approach

Warm rounded shapes, a barista's handwritten notes and a milk-to-chocolate palette, combined with genuinely useful interactions: a menu with search and deep links, and booking on a floor plan.

What works
  • “Anatomy of a cup” — layers of espresso, water, milk and foam in real ratios
  • Menu: 9 categories, 38 items, accessible tabs and search
  • Table booking on a floor plan with capacity and time slots
  • Live open/closed status and breakfast timer based on Kyiv time
  • Gallery with filters and a lightbox
  • Schema.org markup, sitemap and Open Graph
