Premium Automotive

StatusDemo
CategoryWebsites
ClientExample — fictional detailing studio and supercar rental
Year2026
timur-alt2008.github.io/Example-Premium-Automotive-For-my-Portfolio/
Open live demoSource on GitHub
Overview

A multi-page site for a detailing studio with a rental collection of 14 real supercars and luxury cars.

Goal

Combine two businesses — detailing and rentals — in one coherent, fast catalogue with working booking logic.

Approach

A catalogue with URL-synced filters, car pages with spec readouts, a date-range booking calculator and a paint-layer diagram for detailing services.

What works
  • Catalogue filters: brand, body, price range, power, seats
  • Rental booking with live price, discounts and validation
  • 17 detailing services and 3 packages with comparison
  • Before/after slider with mouse, touch and keyboard
  • Page transitions and masked headline reveals
  • Everything off for prefers-reduced-motion
