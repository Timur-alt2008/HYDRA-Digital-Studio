Music Recognition Bot

StatusConcept
CategoryTelegram Bots
ClientConcept
Year2026
Overview

A Telegram bot that recognises a song from a voice message or audio clip and keeps a short history of what you found.

Goal

Shazam-style recognition without leaving the chat, with protection against spam.

Approach

aiogram handlers for voice and audio, a recognition service behind a clean interface, SQLite for each user's last 10 tracks and rate limiting.

What works
  • Voice and audio file recognition
  • History of the last 10 tracks
  • Anti-spam rate limiting
  • Inline buttons for sharing a result
