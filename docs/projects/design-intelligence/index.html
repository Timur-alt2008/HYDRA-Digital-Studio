T-HYDRA Design Intelligence

StatusInternal tool
CategoryDigital Products
ClientT-HYDRA — our own research tool
Year2026
github.com/Timur-alt2008/HYDRA-Design-Intelligence
Source on GitHub
Overview

A website research system: it analyses public sites, extracts design and motion techniques, scores them for T-HYDRA and keeps a searchable reference library.

Goal

Research before pixels: collect proven techniques systematically, filter out other companies' business models and turn the rest into concrete design recommendations.

Approach

Python + Playwright open a site in four viewports, an in-page probe reads tokens, typography, components and motion, and a scoring engine sorts every idea into High priority, Inspiration or Rejected. A local web app manages the library.

What works
  • URL validation with SSRF protection and robots.txt checks
  • Desktop, laptop, tablet and mobile screenshots
  • Design tokens, fonts, components and motion detection
  • Six scores per idea and a design review checklist
  • Library with search, filters, tags, notes and statuses
  • Recommendation engine that generated this site's direction
